# Welcome to ForeBalance

See how the cash flows you enter affect your projected balance, and when it crosses your chosen thresholds. Start with a sample scenario, adjust the plain-text entries, and keep your data in this browser.

Projected balances follow the lines you type. They are not a promise about your real accounts, and the numbers below are not personal financial advice.

Type your own lines on **[Entries](/#entries)**. **[Help](/#help)** is the full syntax. **[About](/#about)** covers access and terms. **[Privacy](/#privacy)** is the local-only promise.
